## Research position

The paper is service-loop-first. The frozen PVC model is the interpretation
component in a continuous sequence of sensing, interpretation, decision,
execution, validation, and feedback. Experiments 0-6 establish the component's
data, cross-domain, automatic-R-peak, false-alarm, and robustness properties;
Experiments 8A and 7 establish the service-chain evidence required for the
paper's principal contribution.

The primary question is whether a simulated ECG acquisition endpoint and local
gateway can form an auditable, recoverable, low-latency, measurable,
non-diagnostic PVC-risk service loop without cloud-dependent inference.

## Contribution order

1. Two-tier local service-loop architecture and responsibility contract.
2. Versioned event contract, provenance, acknowledgement, and local policy.
3. Recovery under loss, reordering, outage, mismatch, and restart conditions.
4. Frozen multi-source PVC interpretation evidence as the application case.
5. Joint clinical and service metrics: detection, latency, recovery,
   auditability, bandwidth, CPU, and memory.

This study addresses architecture/framework, service contracts, event-driven
orchestration, provenance/auditability, error recovery, and closed-loop
benchmarking/simulation topics in the CFP. It does not claim complete
interoperability, deployment security, energy measurement, governance coverage,
or a home/hospital/community pilot.
